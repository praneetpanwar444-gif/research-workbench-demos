01 / Interactive planning simulation

Field Sampling Trade-off Explorer

Illustrative assumptions: INR 4,000 fixed travel cost and capacity of 12 samples per field day.

Planned samples
Spend (INR)

Formulas, assumptions and limits

Affordable = floor(max(0, budget − 4000) / cost). Capacity = 12 × days. Planned = min(affordable, capacity). Spend = 4000 + planned × cost for budgets of at least 4000. Unused = budget − spend.

These are synthetic planning inputs, not prices, measured data or a scientific sample-size/power calculation. Travel is incurred even if no sample is affordable. Site access, safety, laboratory constraints, independence and scientific design require separate review.

How this study tool works

Eight original practice prompts cover basic maths and research documentation. Review marks are a personal study aid, not an assessment score, certificate or earned badge. Practice is not a substitute for domain training.
